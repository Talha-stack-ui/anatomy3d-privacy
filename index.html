Privacy Policy – Anatomy 3D

App: Anatomy 3D – Human Body (Android package com.talha.anatomy3d)
Developer: Talha Ansari
Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Anatomy 3D app uses, why, and the choices you have. Anatomy 3D is a free educational app for learning human anatomy. It is supported by ads.

1. Summary

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or photos.
  • We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and we do not run any server of our own.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Your study progress (for example which bones you have studied) is saved only on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves a small settings file on your phone or computer. It contains your study progress (bones marked as studied), whether you have seen the welcome screen, and timers for features unlocked by watching an ad. This file never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app uses Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, to show banner, video (rewarded) and full-screen ads. To deliver, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

InformationWhy it is used
Advertising ID (Android advertising identifier)Showing ads, limiting how often you see an ad, measuring ads, preventing fraud
Approximate location (from your IP address)Showing ads suitable for your country or region, preventing fraud
Device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language)Showing ads that work on your device, analytics, diagnostics
Interaction with ads (views, taps)Measuring ads and rewarding you when you finish a video
Crash and diagnostic data from the ads SDKKeeping the ads service working

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. It is sent over encrypted connections. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for a general audience. Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and user privacy

4. Your choices and consent

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland: before personalised ads are shown, the app displays Google's consent form, where you can accept or refuse.
  • US states with privacy laws (for example California): you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising through the same Google form.
  • You can change your choice at any time with “Privacy settings (ads)” in the app (Help screen and Layers tab), where it applies to your region.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu depends on your phone).
  • Choosing non-personalised ads does not stop ads from showing, but they will be less related to your interests.

5. Children

Anatomy 3D is intended for students and adults learning anatomy (for example medical, dental, nursing and physiotherapy students). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. The only third party that receives device information is Google, for the advertising purposes described in section 3.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies; you can manage it through the choices in section 4 and in your Google account settings.

8. Permissions

The Android app uses the internet and network-state permissions only to load ads, and the advertising ID permission for the purposes in section 3. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

9. Educational content

Anatomy 3D is for learning only. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment. 3D anatomy data: BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science (CC BY 4.0 and CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan).

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
